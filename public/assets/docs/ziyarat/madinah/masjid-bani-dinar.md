<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Bani Dinar is one of the historical mosques of Madinah associated with the tribe of Banu Dinar, an Ansari tribe that supported Prophet Muhammad ﷺ after the Hijrah. The mosque reflects the strong relationship between the Prophet ﷺ and the tribes of Madinah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Following the migration to Madinah, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ frequently visited different tribes to strengthen unity among Muslims. Historical records mention his visits to Banu Dinar, where prayers were offered and Islamic teachings were delivered. The mosque later became known as Masjid Bani Dinar, preserving the memory of these important interactions.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Bani Dinar symbolizes the unity between the Muhajirun and Ansar. It reflects the role of the Ansar in supporting Islam and demonstrates how neighbourhood mosques became centres of worship, education, and community life during the Prophet's ﷺ era.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Although the original structure has undergone changes over the centuries, the location remains recognised as one of Madinah's historical mosques.</p>
