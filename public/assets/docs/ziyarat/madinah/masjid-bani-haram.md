<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Bani Haram is one of the historical mosques associated with the tribe of Banu Haram in Madinah. It is remembered as a place where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ visited and offered prayers during his interactions with the local Muslim community.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The mosque derives its name from the tribe of Banu Haram, one of the Ansari tribes of Madinah. Historical reports mention that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ visited this area and maintained close relations with its people. The mosque later became a symbol of the tribe's contribution to the early Islamic community.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Bani Haram highlights the important role played by the Ansar in supporting Islam. It reminds visitors of the strong relationship between the Prophet ﷺ and the people of Madinah, whose hospitality and sacrifice helped establish the first Islamic society.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Although rebuilt several times, the location continues to be recognised among Madinah's historical mosques and remains part of historical Ziyarat routes.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Location</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Name: Masjid Bani Haram</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">City: Madinah</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Near Mount Sila', close to the Seven Mosques.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Included in historical heritage tours. Visitors should maintain silence and respect. No specific acts of worship are prescribed. Easily accessible by road.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Named after the Ansari tribe Banu Haram. Closely connected with the events surrounding the Battle of the Trench. Represents the contribution of Madinah's tribes to early Islam. Located near several famous Islamic landmarks.</p>
