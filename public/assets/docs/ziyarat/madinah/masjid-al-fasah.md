<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Al-Fasah is a historical mosque located near Jabal Uhud in Madinah. It is associated with the events of the Battle of Uhud and is considered one of the important historical sites connected with the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his companions.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Al-Fasah is traditionally linked with the area where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ stayed during the Battle of Uhud. Historical accounts mention that after the battle, the Prophet ﷺ spent time in this region while caring for the injured and arranging matters related to the Muslim army. The mosque represents the locations connected with the movements and activities of the Prophet ﷺ during the Battle of Uhud.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The importance of Masjid Al-Fasah comes from its connection with the Seerah (life) of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Visiting such historical places allows Muslims to reflect upon the hardships faced by the Prophet ﷺ and his companions while spreading Islam. The site serves as a reminder of patience, sacrifice, and devotion shown by the early Muslim community.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The original structure of the historical mosque has undergone changes over time. The location remains recognised as part of the Uhud historical area and is visited by people interested in Islamic heritage.</p>

<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>

<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">SITE TODAY</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Present-day condition Today, Masjid al-Fasah is seen as a historical site at the foot of Mount Uhud. It is not a large active landmark, but a place visitors notice because of what it represents. The mosque’s small scale makes it easy to pass by, yet its importance grows when its story is understood. Physical appearance The surviving remains are modest and weathered. The structure has been described as partly ruined, with traces of walls and a visible mihrab in some reports. An iron fence has been used to protect the site, which shows that it is treated as a preserved historical remnant rather than a rebuilt modern mosque. Visitor setting Visitors usually encounter the site as part of a broader trip to Uhud Mountain and the surrounding historical area. The mosque sits in a quiet location near other important places connected with the battle. Because of that, it is often visited with reflection rather than with long stops or formal ceremonies. Respect and preservation Masjid al-Fasah should be approached with the same dignity given to other places tied to the Seerah. It is not a decorative attraction. It is a historical reminder of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺthe Companions رضي الله عنهمand the lived geography of Madinah. Respectful behavior and calm attention suit the spirit of the place.</p>
