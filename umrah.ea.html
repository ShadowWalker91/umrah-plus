How long are you staying?

Pick the number of nights you have. The itinerary below reshapes to match, so you can see how each length actually feels day by day.

5 nights: Long weekend

3 nights Makkah, 2 nights Madinah

Enough for Umrah, one full day of Ziyarat in each city, and time in the Haram without rushing.

A sample itinerary for 5 nights

  1. Day 1 · Makkah

    Fly to Jeddah (Makkah-first) or Madinah (Madinah-first), transfer to your hotel

    Meet at the airport in the UAE, ihram before landing, private transfer to your hotel and, if you wish, your first Umrah that evening.

  2. Day 2 · Makkah

    Umrah and rest

    Complete Umrah with the group or on your own. The rest of the day is yours.

  3. Day 3 · Makkah

    Makkah Ziyarat

    Jabal al-Nour, Jabal Thawr, Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah with a guide who explains what you are seeing.

  4. Day 4 · Madinah

    Travel to Madinah, Madinah Ziyarat

    Morning train or car to Madinah, then Masjid Quba, Masjid Qiblatain and Mount Uhud in the afternoon. Maghrib at the Prophet’s Mosque.

  5. Day 5 · Madinah

    Return home

    Fajr and a quiet morning at Masjid an-Nabawi, then transfer to Madinah airport and fly back to the UAE.

A typical shape for the journey. Yours will follow the dates and pace you choose.

Questions about flying

All questions and answers
  • How long does an Umrah visa take from the UAE?

    Usually 3 to 5 working days once we have your documents. We recommend applying about three weeks before you travel so there is room for any query from the Saudi side.

  • How early should I book my Umrah?

    Three to four weeks ahead is comfortable for most dates. For Ramadan, Eid and school holidays, book six to eight weeks ahead: hotels near the Haram fill early and prices rise.

  • Do you publish prices?

    No. Prices move with dates, hotels and group size, so a fixed number online is usually wrong by the time you read it. Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and who is travelling, and we send a clear written quote the same day.

  • Which airports do you fly from?

    Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi are all standard departure options. Flights land in Jeddah for Makkah-first itineraries or Madinah for Madinah-first ones.

  • How far are the hotels from the Haram?

    It depends on the tier. Premium hotels are within about 300 metres of the Haram, Standard within roughly 800 metres, and Economy hotels are a short shuttle or 15 minute walk away. Each tier states the distance on the package page.

  • Do you book the Haramain high-speed train?

    Yes. We can add Haramain train tickets between Jeddah, Makkah and Madinah to any package. It is faster and calmer than the road, and we handle the booking and seat allocation.
