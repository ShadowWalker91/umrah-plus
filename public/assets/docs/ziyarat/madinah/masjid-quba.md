<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Quba is the first mosque established in Islam. It was founded by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ upon his arrival near Madinah during the Hijrah. The Prophet ﷺ personally participated in laying its foundation, making it one of Islam's most sacred historical landmarks.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">When Prophet Muhammad ﷺ migrated from Makkah, he stayed in Quba for several days before entering Madinah. During this stay, he established Masjid Quba with the assistance of his Companions. The mosque is mentioned in relation to the Quranic description of a mosque founded upon righteousness from the first day.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Quba holds exceptional virtue in Islam. Authentic Hadith mention that performing two Rak'ahs of prayer in Masjid Quba after purification carries the reward of an Umrah. It remains one of the most important Ziyarat sites in Madinah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Today, Masjid Quba is a large and beautifully expanded mosque capable of accommodating thousands of worshippers while preserving its historic identity.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Location</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Name: Masjid Quba</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">City: Madinah</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Quba District, approximately 3–4 km south of Masjid an-Nabawi.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Open 24 hours. Separate prayer areas for men and women. Accessible by road and the Quba Walk. Best visited during early morning or evening.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">First mosque built in Islamic history. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ regularly visited it, often on Saturdays. Mentioned in the Holy Quran regarding righteousness. One of the most visited mosques after Masjid an-Nabawi.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Quba is one of the most beloved places in Madinah because it is remembered as the first mosque built in Islam. That simple fact gives it a weight that many visitors feel immediately. It is not only a historic building. It is the beginning of a physical place of worship in the new home of Islam after the Hijrah. For pilgrims, that makes the mosque deeply meaningful, especially when they are already walking through the sacred history of Madinah. The mosque is closely tied to the arrival of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in Madinah. He stayed in Quba during the early days of the Hijrah, and this period became one of the most important turning points in Islamic history. The place carries the memory of new beginnings, but also of patience, migration, and the careful building of a community. That is why Masjid Quba is not only visited as a landmark. It is visited as part of the Seerah itself. For many people, the beauty of this mosque is that it joins history and worship in a very direct way. Visitors come for prayer, reflection, and remembrance. They also come because the mosque helps them understand how Islam took root in Madinah. In the wider map of Madinah Ziyarat, Masjid Quba stands near the beginning of the story. It is one of the clearest places where faith, place, and memory meet.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The first mosque in Islam Masjid Quba is remembered as the first mosque built in Islam. That makes it one of the most important historical places in Madinah and in the wider Muslim world. Its value is not tied to size or decoration. It lies in what it represents: the first dedicated house of worship established after the Hijrah, when the Muslim community entered a new phase of life in Madinah. This first mosque gave the new community a place to gather, pray, and begin life in a settled and visible way. It was a sign that the Muslims were no longer only traveling between places; they were now forming a community with a center of worship. That is a deeply moving part of Islamic history, because it shows how the life of faith was built carefully and purposefully from the very beginning.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The Prophet’s stay in Quba Masjid Quba is closely linked to the early days when Prophet Muhammad ﷺ arrived in the Madinah region from Makkah. Classical and later historical memory connect the mosque with his stay in Quba before entering the main city. This connection gives the site its special place in Seerah. It is remembered not as a random stop, but as part of the first moments of the Prophet’s ﷺ life in Madinah. That early period was full of change. The Hijrah had brought the Muslims out of Makkah, and Quba became one of the first places where the new chapter of Madinan life began to take shape. For visitors, this makes the mosque feel close to the story of migration itself. It is a reminder that Islam’s history was lived through real journeys, real arrivals, and real acts of worship.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">A place of prayer and continuity Masjid Quba is not only remembered for its beginning; it is also remembered for what it continued to mean. Later generations treated it as a mosque worth visiting and honoring. The tradition of praying there remained alive because the site was connected to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and to the early growth of the Muslim community. That continuity is part of what gives the mosque its warmth. The mosque’s importance was preserved through centuries of attention, rebuilding, and care. Over time, it became one of the central places to visit in Madinah for people who want to understand the city’s sacred geography. Its continued role shows how Muslims have protected places that remind them of the earliest days of Islam. In this way, Masjid Quba remains both historical and living.</p>

<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">SITE TODAY</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Present-day identity Today, Masjid Quba is a major and widely visited mosque in Madinah. It remains a place where worship and history meet naturally. Even with its modern structure, the mosque still carries the feeling of an early sacred beginning. Visitors often come here as part of a wider Madinah Ziyarat route. Physical appearance The mosque is well known for its large, clean, and carefully maintained appearance. Its present form reflects modern expansion and preservation while still honoring its heritage. The setting feels open and welcoming, which suits the mosque’s role as a place of prayer and reflection. Visitor experience Many visitors arrive here with a sense of reverence because they know they are standing at one of the most important sites in Islamic history. Some come briefly, others stay longer for prayer and quiet reflection. The experience is usually calm, respectful, and spiritually focused.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">WHY IT STILL MATTERS</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Quba still matters because it reminds Muslims that the life of the faith was built step by step. Before there were large institutions, there was a mosque. Before there was a settled city life, there was migration, patience, and prayer. That is what makes the site so moving. It shows how the early Muslim community began with worship at its center. For today’s visitor, Masjid Quba offers more than history. It offers perspective. It teaches that sacred growth often begins quietly, with sincere effort and trust in Allah. In Madinah Ziyarat, it is one of the places that helps the heart understand how the Seerah became a living city.</p>
