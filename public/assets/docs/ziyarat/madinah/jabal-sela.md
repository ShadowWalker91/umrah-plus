<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Jabal Sela is one of the most historically significant mountains in Madinah. It is best known for its association with the Battle of the Trench (Ghazwat al-Khandaq), where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his Companions defended Madinah against a large coalition of tribes. The mountain remains one of the most important landmarks for understanding early Islamic military history.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">In 5 AH, during the Battle of the Trench, Jabal Sela served as a strategic defensive position for the Muslim army. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ supervised the construction of the trench and the defense of the city from this area. Several nearby historical mosques, including the Seven Mosques and Masjid al-Rayah, are closely connected with the events that took place around Jabal Sela.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Jabal Sela symbolizes faith, unity, and perseverance. It reminds Muslims of the courage and determination shown by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his Companions during one of the most difficult moments in Islamic history.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The mountain remains preserved as a natural landmark and is frequently visited during historical Ziyarat tours. Modern pathways allow visitors to view the surrounding historical sites connected with the Battle of the Trench.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Location</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Name: Jabal Sela (Mount Sila')</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">City: Madinah</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Western side of Masjid an-Nabawi, near the Seven Mosques.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Easily accessible by road. Best visited with a historical guide. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended. No specific acts of worship are prescribed.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Strategic location during the Battle of the Trench. Overlooks the area where the trench was dug. Surrounded by several important historical mosques. One of Madinah's most important historical mountains.</p>
