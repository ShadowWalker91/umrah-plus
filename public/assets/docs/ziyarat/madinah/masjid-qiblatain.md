<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Qiblatain is one of the most famous historical mosques in Islam. It is renowned as the place where the command was revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ to change the Qiblah from Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem to the Ka'bah in Makkah during prayer.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">In the second year after Hijrah, while leading a congregational prayer, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ received Divine revelation instructing him to face the Ka'bah. The Prophet ﷺ immediately turned during the prayer, and the congregation followed him. This historic event gave the mosque its name, Masjid Qiblatain, meaning "The Mosque of the Two Qiblahs."</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Qiblatain marks one of the most significant events in Islamic history. The change of Qiblah established the Ka'bah as the permanent direction of prayer for Muslims worldwide and reinforced the distinct identity of the Muslim Ummah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The mosque has been beautifully renovated and is one of the most visited Islamic landmarks in Madinah. It is maintained by the Saudi authorities and welcomes thousands of visitors every year.</p>

<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Open daily. Separate prayer areas for men and women. Accessible by road. Appropriate Islamic dress is required.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Famous as the Mosque of Two Qiblahs. Marks the change of Qiblah during Salah. One of the most visited mosques in Madinah.</p>
