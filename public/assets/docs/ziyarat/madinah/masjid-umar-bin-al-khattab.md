<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Umar bin Al-Khattab is a historical mosque in Madinah named after the second Caliph of Islam, Umar ibn Al-Khattab (R.A.). The mosque commemorates his close association with the Prophet ﷺ and his significant role in the establishment and administration of the Islamic state.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Historical traditions associate the mosque with Umar ibn Al-Khattab (R.A.), one of the Prophet's ﷺ closest Companions. Although the present structure has undergone reconstruction, the site has long been recognized among Madinah's historical mosques and reflects the legacy of one of Islam's greatest leaders.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The mosque honors the memory of Umar ibn Al-Khattab (R.A.), renowned for his justice, leadership, and devotion to Islam. Visiting the mosque offers an opportunity to reflect upon his contributions to the growth and stability of the Muslim Ummah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Umar bin Al-Khattab has been renovated over time and remains an active place of worship. It is occasionally included in historical Ziyarat tours of Madinah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Location</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Name: Masjid Umar bin Al-Khattab (R.A.)</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">City: Madinah</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Historical district near Masjid an-Nabawi.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Open for daily prayers. Accessible through Madinah's central road network. Visitors should observe proper Islamic etiquette. Usually visited together with nearby historical mosques.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Named after the second Rightly Guided Caliph. Reflects the legacy of Umar ibn Al-Khattab (R.A.). Included among Madinah's historical mosques. Associated with the early development of the Islamic state.</p>
