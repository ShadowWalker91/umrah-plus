<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The Seven Mosques (Sab'ah Masajid) are among the most visited historical landmarks in Madinah. The complex consists of several small mosques built near the site of the Battle of the Trench (Ghazwat al-Khandaq). These mosques commemorate important locations associated with Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his Companions during the defence of Madinah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The Battle of the Trench took place in 5 AH when the Muslims defended Madinah against a large coalition of tribes. Several mosques were later constructed to mark locations connected with the Prophet ﷺ and prominent Companions, including Abu Bakr (R.A.), Umar (R.A.), Ali (R.A.), Salman al-Farsi (R.A.), and Fatimah (R.A.). Although commonly called the "Seven Mosques," the exact number of structures has changed over time because of reconstruction and urban development.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The Seven Mosques preserve the memory of one of the greatest defensive victories in Islamic history. They remind visitors of the faith, sacrifice, and unity of the early Muslim community during a time of immense difficulty.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Today, the mosque complex is maintained as one of Madinah's major historical attractions. Some original structures have been restored, while others have been incorporated into modern developments.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Location</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Name: Seven Masajid (Sab'ah Masajid)</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">City: Madinah</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Western side of Mount Sila', near the Battle of the Trench site.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Open throughout the year. Usually included in all historical Ziyarat tours. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended. Respect local regulations while visiting.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Closely connected with the Battle of the Trench. Includes mosques linked to several prominent Companions. One of the most photographed historical sites in Madinah.</p>
