<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Bani Anif is one of the historical mosques linked to the tribe of Banu Anif in Madinah. It represents the spread of Islam among the local tribes and reflects the role of neighborhood mosques in strengthening the Muslim community during the Prophet's ﷺ lifetime.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The mosque was established in the area inhabited by Banu Anif, one of the tribes connected with early Islamic Madinah. Historical sources indicate that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ maintained close relations with various tribes, encouraging unity, worship, and community development. Masjid Bani Anif later became a reminder of those interactions.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Masjid Bani Anif symbolizes the unity and brotherhood established between the Prophet ﷺ and the people of Madinah. It reflects the importance of local mosques as centers of worship, education, and social cohesion during the formative years of Islam.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Although the original structure has undergone significant changes over the centuries, the location continues to be recognised among Madinah's historical religious sites.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Historical district of Madinah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Can be visited during historical Ziyarat tours. Visitors should observe respectful behavior. No specific religious rituals are associated with the mosque. Often combined with visits to nearby heritage sites.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Named after the tribe of Banu Anif. Represents the expansion of Islam among Madinah's tribes. Reflects the importance of community mosques in early Islamic society. Preserved as part of Madinah's historical heritage.</p>
