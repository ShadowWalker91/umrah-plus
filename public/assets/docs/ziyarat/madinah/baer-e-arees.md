<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Bir Arees, also known as the Well of Arees, is one of the most famous historical wells in Madinah. It is closely associated with Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the Rightly Guided Caliphs. The well is particularly remembered because the Prophet's ﷺ silver ring, later used by Abu Bakr (R.A.) and Umar (R.A.), was accidentally lost in it during the caliphate of Uthman ibn Affan (R.A.).</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Historical Background</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Bir Arees was frequently visited by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, who rested near the well with his Companions. During the caliphate of Uthman ibn Affan (R.A.), the Prophet's ﷺ seal ring accidentally fell into the well and could not be recovered despite extensive efforts. This incident became one of the best-known historical events associated with the well.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Islamic Significance</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Bir Arees is significant because of its close association with Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the Khulafa al-Rashidun. It reminds Muslims of the simplicity of the Prophet's ﷺ life and the historical continuity of Islamic leadership after his passing.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Present Status</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The original well has been preserved as part of Madinah's Islamic heritage. Although the surrounding area has undergone modernization, Bir Arees remains a recognised historical landmark.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Location</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Name: Bir Arees (Well of Arees)</h3>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">City: Madinah</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Area: Near Masjid Quba, southern Madinah.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Visitor Information</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">Frequently included in historical Ziyarat tours. Visitors should respect the heritage site. No specific worship is prescribed. Best visited together with Masjid Quba.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px; color: #F9C344;">Interesting Facts</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 12px; line-height: 1.7;">The Prophet's ﷺ seal ring was lost in this well. Associated with all three of the first Rightly Guided Caliphs. One of the best-known historical wells of Madinah. Located close to Masjid Quba.</p>
